AER designates Long Run Exploration Ltd.'s sites to Orphan Well Association for closure

Published April 16, 2026 · Source: BOE Report
The AER has designated Long Run Exploration Ltd.'s wells and facilities to the Orphan Well Association following the operator's insolvency, adding to the orphan inventory that funded industry participants ultimately pay to clean up.
